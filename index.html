About

Malak is a Bahrain-based data scientist and business analyst with a BSc in Data Science and Business Analytics from the University of London, delivered at the Bahrain Institute of Banking and Finance, alongside a Data Science Bootcamp with General Assembly. Her work spans regression and classification modelling, machine learning with Random Forest and XGBoost, and dashboard design in Tableau — always grounded in turning dense, messy datasets into insights that hold up under scrutiny.

Beyond the notebook, she has built her leadership and communication skills through Toastmasters International, serving as club president and secretary, and gained early industry exposure interning with Tamkeen's national workforce development programs. She's driven by the moment a model or a dashboard shifts how a team sees a problem.
